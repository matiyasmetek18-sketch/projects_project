Settings

The score blends three numbers, each from 0 to 100: impact, portfolio, and feasibility. These are author judgments, not measurements. The percentage is how much of the blend that part counts for.

By default the score is 45% of the impact number, plus 25% of the portfolio number, plus 30% of the feasibility number, rounded to a whole number. The fields below set those shares. If they do not sum to 100, they are scaled so they do. The score is recomputed on the next request from the current weights and is not stored. Dismissed ideas stay off the ranked feed.
