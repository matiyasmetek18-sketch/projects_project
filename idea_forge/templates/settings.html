Impact, portfolio, and feasibility are author judgments on a 0 to 100 scale. They are not measurements. Weights change how those judgments combine and take effect on the next request.

The default is 45 / 25 / 30, which is impact × 0.45 + portfolio × 0.25 + feasibility × 0.30. If the three weights do not sum to 100, they are renormalized. The score is rounded to an integer and is not stored. Dismissed ideas stay out of the ranked feed.
